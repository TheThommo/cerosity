# Equipment Standards in Golf

## Governance Framework

The R&A and USGA jointly set equipment standards through the Equipment Rules, which define the specifications that clubs, balls, and other equipment must meet to be conforming under the Rules of Golf. Both organisations maintain testing facilities and publish lists of conforming equipment.

## Key Equipment Regulations

**Clubs:**
- Maximum of 14 clubs allowed in the bag during a round (Rule 4.1b)
- Driver head volume limited to 460cc
- Club length limited to 46 inches (since 2022 Model Local Rule)
- Groove specifications regulate spin generation on iron clubfaces
- Putter anchoring (fixing the club against the body) prohibited since 2016

**Balls:**
- Maximum initial velocity, overall distance standard, and symmetry requirements
- Diameter minimum 1.680 inches, weight maximum 1.620 ounces
- The R&A and USGA publish a Conforming Golf Ball List updated regularly

**Other equipment:**
- Distance-measuring devices permitted by default since 2019 (committees may prohibit)
- Green-reading materials limited to specific sizes and scales
- Alignment aids and training devices restricted during competition

## Model Local Rule for Ball Distance

In 2023, the R&A and USGA announced a Model Local Rule allowing elite competitions to require a ball that travels shorter distances. This addresses concerns about the increasing length of professional golfers and the impact on course design and sustainability. Implementation is expected from 2026 at elite competition level only.

## Mental Performance Connection

Equipment decisions are a common source of Red Head distraction. Constant club changes, chasing distance with new drivers, or believing equipment is holding you back — these are avoidance behaviours that shift focus from skill development to material solutions.

Blue Head approach: choose conforming equipment that fits your game, commit to it, and focus on what you can control — your technique, strategy, and mental approach. Equipment is a tool, not a solution.

*Source: R&A/USGA Equipment Rules 2023; R&A Conforming Equipment Lists; R&A/USGA joint statement on distance 2023*
