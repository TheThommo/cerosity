# Se Ri Pak

**Era:** 1990s–2010s | **Role:** Professional golfer | **Nationality:** South Korean

## Bio Summary

Se Ri Pak won 25 LPGA Tour events including five Major championships and is credited with single-handedly igniting the Korean golf revolution. Her victories in 1998 — winning the LPGA Championship and the US Women's Open as a 20-year-old rookie — inspired an entire generation of South Korean women to take up golf.

Pak's US Women's Open victory at Blackwolf Run is one of the most dramatic in championship history. She played a sudden-death playoff and then a Monday 18-hole playoff, famously removing her shoes to play a shot from a water hazard in the process. The image was broadcast across South Korea and became a cultural landmark.

By the time Pak retired in 2016, over 40 South Korean women had won on the LPGA Tour — a direct consequence of her inspiration. She was inducted into the World Golf Hall of Fame in 2007 at age 30, the youngest living inductee at that time.
